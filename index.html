ABOUT US

Gramin Bharat TV

25+ Years of Dedicated Cinematic Contribution, Social Empowerment & Rural Storytelling.

25+

Years In Cinema

Director & Producer

Vilas Gadge (विलास गाडगे)

Over the past 25+ years, we have successfully completed 10 to 15 Marathi and Hindi films, documentaries, and socially driven projects. This journey, which began from zero, has grown into one marked by trust, experience, and a strong, credible identity.

Through film screenings aimed at public awareness in rural areas, various programs conducted at the Gram Panchayat level, awareness campaigns implemented during the COVID period, and direct interaction with villagers with active community participation, our work has never been limited to the screen alone. It is deeply connected to the soil, the people, and the realities of their everyday lives.
